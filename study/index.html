How the posting side works
  • One artefact per module, posted the week you finish it: a repo, a notebook, a chart or a short write-up. Cross-post to LinkedIn and X with the code linked.
  • Write for the person who made the video. Tag them when you build on their material; creators reshare good derived work, and that is how you get in front of their audience.
  • Your own research is the strongest material you have. The reward hacking results and the cross-model memory work are rarer than anything in this course, so they get the biggest posts.
  • Comment with substance on Interconnects, the RLHF book Discord and AI Engineer talk threads. Ten useful replies beat one cold DM.
  • By module 6, pitch a talk. AI Engineer runs open calls for speakers, and London meetups are an easier first stage.
